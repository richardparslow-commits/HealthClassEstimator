Legal disclaimer. This tool is not a medical diagnostic tool and does not issue insurance, bind coverage, or replace a carrier underwriter’s decision. Never suggest withholding information or “answering around” a condition: applications state that answers influence acceptance and that material misrepresentation or nondisclosure can jeopardize coverage. Temporary coverage exists only if the exact carrier receipt conditions are met, not because this tool gives a favorable estimate.
